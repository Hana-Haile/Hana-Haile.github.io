Relationship Between COVID-19 and Pathology Results

This dashboard explores differences in pathology results between patients with and without COVID-19. It compares five pathology measures: ferritin, troponin, neutrophils, lymphocytes and CRP. Results can also be examined across age groups, gender and ethnicity using weighted averages.
